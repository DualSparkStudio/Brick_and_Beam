Brick and Beam — Luxury 4BHK Villa in Mahabaleshwar

Escape to timeless luxury in the Western Ghats. Brick and Beam is an opulent 4BHK private villa in Bhilar, Mahabaleshwar blending tranquil nature with refined comfort, breathtaking valley views, warm hospitality, and private spaces designed for families and groups.

About Brick and Beam Villa

Nestled between Panchgani and Mahabaleshwar in scenic Bhilar, Brick and Beam offers an exclusive holiday retreat. With 4 spacious air-conditioned bedrooms, private balconies overlooking green hills, and expansive living and dining areas, guests enjoy ultimate seclusion, tranquility, and first-class comfort.

Villa Amenities & Facilities

  • 4BHK Luxury Villa: 4 spacious air-conditioned bedrooms with attached Western-style bathrooms, fresh linen, and extra mattresses.
  • Swimming Pool: 13 ft × 26 ft private swimming pool with a dedicated kids pool.
  • Kitchen: Fully equipped modern kitchen for self-catering or special culinary requests.
  • Connectivity & Comfort: High-speed Wi-Fi, 24-hour hot water, and a high-fidelity music system.
  • Parking & Security: Secure gated private on-site parking.
  • Scenic Outdoor Spaces: Bonfire area, sun deck, open-plan lounge, and private valley-view balconies.
